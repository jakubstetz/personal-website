Coaching

I serve clients as a transformational coach. What is coaching, exactly? I appreciate the definition given by the International Coaching Federation:

"Partnering with clients in a thought-provoking and creative process that inspires them to maximize their personal and professional potential. The process of coaching often unlocks previously untapped sources of imagination, productivity and leadership."

As a coach, it is my mission to support you in courageous introspection and inspired action by listening to you in a way that no one else listens to you, asking you the questions that no one else will ask you, and challenging you in a way that no one else will challenge you. I am enthusiastic to coach around any topic that might be pertinent for you: finances, health, relationships (whether friendly, familial, professional, or romantnic), personal projects, professional trajectory, home life, spiritual life, creativity... My particular specialities are spirituality, philosophy of life, and communication.

I am a certified Associate Transformational Coach through the Satvatove Institute, and I am currently working towards certification as an Associate Certified Coach through the International Coaching Federation. I coach according to the Satvatove Values, Principles and Codes and the International Coaching Federation Core Values and Code of Ethics.

Because I believe that the best way for you to reach a decision on whether you would like to coach with me is by experiencing my coaching first-hand, I conduct my first session with new clients at no cost.
